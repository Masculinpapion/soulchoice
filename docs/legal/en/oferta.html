2. Description of the service and plans

SoulChoice is a digital service for finding company for events (restaurants, bars, concerts, travel). The basic features of the service are free; each user is given 3 free responses to invitations (non-renewable).

The paid plan is "SoulChoice Premium": 1 000 ₽ per 30 days. The plan includes an unlimited number of responses to invitations; the plan contains no other paid features.

The plan can be paid for in one of two ways:

  • Subscription with auto-renewal: payment by bank card; every 30 days 1 000 ₽ is automatically charged to the linked card until the user cancels the subscription. No later than 24 hours before the next charge, the service notifies the user by push notification and/or by e-mail to the address provided at checkout.
  • One-time payment: a single payment for 30 days of access (bank card or SBP) without auto-renewal.

Prices are stated in Russian roubles. Not subject to VAT due to the application of a special tax regime.

2.1. Auto-renewal, cancellation of the subscription and card linking

When taking out a subscription, the user confirms agreement with the auto-renewal terms by ticking the corresponding box and provides an e-mail for notifications and fiscal receipts. The first charge is made at the moment the subscription is taken out; each subsequent charge — 30 days after the previous one.

The subscription can be cancelled at any time without giving reasons: in the application (Profile → Subscription → "Cancel subscription") or on the website soulchoice.app/premium after signing in with your phone number. Cancellation is free of charge and takes effect immediately: further charges stop, and access is retained until the end of the period already paid for.

Cancelling the subscription stops automatic charges; payment data (the card token) is held by the acquiring bank and is not accessible to the service. To fully remove the card link, the user may contact support@soulchoice.app.

If a scheduled charge fails (insufficient funds, card blocked, etc.), the service notifies the user and retries within 2 days; access is retained during the retries and the grace period. If the charge still fails, auto-renewal is switched off and access ends at the end of the grace period.

The grace period is 24 hours after the last charge attempt. Deleting the account switches off auto-renewal; the unused balance is refunded upon request.

2.2. Price changes

The service notifies the user of a change in the subscription price at least 7 days before the next charge. A user who does not agree with the new price may cancel the subscription before the charge date; in that case the new price does not apply to them.

3. Payment methods

Bank cards of the МИР, VISA, MasterCard payment systems are accepted, as well as payment via the Faster Payments System (SBP).

A subscription with auto-renewal can be taken out only with a bank card; SBP is available for one-time payments. When taking out a subscription, the card is saved (tokenised) on the bank's side.

Payments are processed through a secure payment gateway in accordance with the PCI DSS security standard. Bank card data is transmitted in encrypted form via SSL/TLS and is not accessible to the seller. If the card is enrolled in 3-D Secure, the user is automatically redirected to the issuing bank's page for additional authentication.

4. Procedure for providing the service

The service is digital and is provided remotely throughout the territory of the Russian Federation and beyond. Access to the plan's features is activated automatically within a few minutes after payment is confirmed.

No physical delivery of goods is made; no additional fee is charged for providing access.

5. Refunds and withdrawal from the service

In accordance with the Law of the Russian Federation "On Protection of Consumer Rights", the user has the right to withdraw from the service at any time.

  • If the paid features of the subscription were not used in the current paid period, the funds for that period are refunded in full upon request to support@soulchoice.app.
  • If the service has been partially rendered, the refund is made less the cost of the part actually rendered.
  • The refund is made to the same bank card from which the payment was made, within 1 to 30 business days depending on the issuing bank.
  • Auto-renewal is cancelled by the user independently in the application or on the website soulchoice.app/premium and does not require contacting support.

The request is sent to the support address stating the phone number of the account and is reviewed within 10 days; the refund is processed through the acquiring bank, and access to the plan ends on the refund date. Fees for automatic renewal are refunded on the same terms. If the account is blocked for a violation of the Terms of Use, the fee for the current period is not refunded.
