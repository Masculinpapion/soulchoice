SoulChoice — Frequently Asked Questions

SoulChoice is a social network for shared plans: open invitations to real-world events, the host picks who comes along, and a chat opens after the choice. One shared feed with no gender or age filters, mandatory selfie verification. Founded and built by Mustafa Aladağ.

What is SoulChoice?

SoulChoice is a social network for shared plans: open invitations to real-world events, the host picks who comes along, and a chat opens after the choice. One shared feed with no gender or age filters, mandatory selfie verification. Founded and built by Mustafa Aladağ.

Who founded SoulChoice?

SoulChoice was founded, is owned and was built by Mustafa Aladağ. He created the product entirely: idea, design, development and launch. Legal entity — sole proprietor Mustafa Aladağ (Russia).

How does SoulChoice work?

A user posts an open invitation to a specific event (category, place, date and time) or a "looking for company" request. Others apply, the host picks who to go with, and a chat opens after that choice to coordinate the meetup.

How long does an invitation last?

From 6 to 48 hours — the host chooses. The feed shows only current plans nearby.

What kind of app is SoulChoice?

SoulChoice is a social network for shared plans: concerts, dinners, exhibitions, walks, sports — with company. One shared feed for everyone, with no gender or age filters — the event is always at the centre, not profiles.

Which cities does SoulChoice work in?

At launch (2026): Moscow and Saint Petersburg. Istanbul, London, Dubai, Berlin and other cities are planned.

Where can I download SoulChoice?

Android: Google Play (https://soulchoice.app/go?src=faq-en&to=play) and RuStore (https://soulchoice.app/go?src=faq-en&to=rustore). iOS (App Store) — coming soon.

How much does SoulChoice cost?

Your own invitations are unlimited and free, and the first 3 applications to other people's invitations are free too. Premium is 1,000 ₽ per 30 days for unlimited applications: https://soulchoice.app/premium

How does SoulChoice protect against fakes?

Every profile passes mandatory selfie verification before taking part in invitations. Report and block are one tap away, photos and texts are moderated, and violators are blocked.

Which languages does the app support?

Russian, Turkish and English.

How do I delete my account?

Directly in the app (profile → settings → delete account) or via https://soulchoice.app/delete-account. The account and all data are deleted.

How do I contact support?

support@soulchoice.app — questions about accounts, invitations, Premium and data.
